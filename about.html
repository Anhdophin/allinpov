About Me

I'm a Creative Media Designer with a background in Graphic Design since 2002 and experience working in in-house enviroment. Able to work independently, with a focus on creating visual, media, and communication assets for marketing and business communication.

Able to understand briefs, analyze requirements, organize information, develop concepts, and execute production, translating communication needs into appropriate design and media outputs.

Strong in Graphic Design, Marketing Collateral, Information Design, and Media Production, while currently expanding into Creative Video, 360º Media, and Digital Showrooms to create more engaging forms of digital communication and user experiences.

I approach design by understanding what a business needs to communicate, promote, present, or build as an asset, then translate those requirements into practical visual, media, and communication outputs.
